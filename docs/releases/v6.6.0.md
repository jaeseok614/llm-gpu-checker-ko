# v6.6.0 — Guided decisions and trustworthy discovery

v6.6 completes the beginner-first flow introduced in v6.0 while keeping detailed sizing available for review.

## What changed

- v6.1 collapses the task chooser after entry and displays only one easy-estimate step at a time.
- v6.2 reduces the initial result to economy, recommended, and scalable options with the six metrics needed for a first decision.
- v6.3 distinguishes model-specific official sources, official family pages, external references, and missing sources. It also shows Korean price coverage without presenting assumptions as live prices.
- v6.4 adds typo-tolerant aliases and intent search for GPU memory, form factor, vendors, and Korean workload names.
- v6.5 introduces a dedicated Korean price report form, duplicate acknowledgements, and links to open or completed catalog requests.
- v6.6 separates catalog search, evidence policy, and guided CSS into focused modules and covers them in syntax, static-build, and smoke tests.
- v6.6 also repairs selector bindings for estimate save, share, export, proposal, benchmark, and launch-recipe actions.

## User flow

1. Choose whether you own a GPU, know a model, or want to size an AI service.
2. Enter only the minimum inputs for that path.
3. Compare three decision options.
4. Open detailed assumptions, a customer summary, or a source/request contribution only when needed.

All infrastructure quantities, throughput, and prices remain planning estimates. Final procurement requires a supplier quote and a representative workload PoC.
